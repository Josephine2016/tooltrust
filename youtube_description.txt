TITLE: ToolTrust — Verified Repair Tools for Computer Village, Ikeja | WarriorHacks 2.0 Demo (2 min)

DESCRIPTION:
Fake tools are eating technicians' money in Computer Village — the Standard Organisation of Nigeria says up to 80% of gadgets sold there may be fake, and a "₦40,000 original" hot-air station two streets away costs ₦55,000. Nobody can verify anything before they pay.

ToolTrust is a mobile web app built WITH and FOR the repair technicians of Ikeja, Lagos:
✅ Browse verified, price-transparent tools (₦, no wahala)
🔍 Check any tool's batch code before you pay — genuine, invalid, or not-in-registry, in 30 seconds
📲 Order in one tap — lands on the supplier's WhatsApp, the way this market already works
🚩 Report counterfeit stalls and warn the community

Built for WarriorHacks 2.0 (theme: solve an issue in your community) — Hackathon Track. Zero backend, works offline on any Android, demo codes below ⬇️
