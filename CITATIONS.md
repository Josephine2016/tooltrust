## 1. Problem-scale statistics (use in Impact section)

**Claim A:** "Nigerians lose billions annually to fake gadgets, with some reports saying up to 40% of tech gadgets in the market are counterfeit."
- **Source:** TechCabal — "How to identify authentic tech gadgets in Nigeria" (Aug 21, 2025)
- **URL:** https://techcabal.com/2025/08/21/how-to-identify-authentic-tech-gadgets-in-nigeria/
- **Devpost phrasing:** "Reporting suggests up to 40% of tech gadgets in the Nigerian market may be counterfeit (TechCabal, 2025)."

**Claim B:** "Computer Village, Lagos — Africa's largest ICT market — contributes ~$2bn (₦612bn) yearly to the economy, and counterfeiting there rose ~50% within three years of a major SON raid; NCC disclosed ~250 million substandard phones sold yearly in Nigeria."
- **Source:** The Guardian Nigeria — "Counterfeiters threaten N612 billion yearly revenue from ICT" (Apr 26, 2017)
- **URL:** https://guardian.ng/technology/counterfeiters-threaten-n612-billion-yearly-revenue-from-ict/
- **Devpost phrasing:** "Computer Village generates about $2bn annually (The Guardian, 2017) — counterfeiting there grew ~50% in three years, and the NCC estimated 250m substandard phones sold yearly in Nigeria."

**Claim C:** "Prices 30–40% below market value are a documented counterfeiting red flag; identical-looking gadgets vary widely between vendors; lack of receipts means zero recourse."
- **Source:** Simplvest — "Computer Village Lagos: How to Avoid Fake Phones and Gadgets" (Nov 19, 2025)
- **URL:** https://www.simplvest.com/how-to-buy-phones-in-computer-village-safely/
- **Devpost phrasing:** "Market guides document 30–40% price spreads as a counterfeit red flag and advise verifying before paying — exactly the behavior ToolTrust encodes (Simplvest, 2025)."

**Claim D:** "SON has been widely reported as claiming up to 80% of phones and gadgets sold at Computer Village may be fake — a figure disputed by the traders' association (CAPDAN), which itself confirms counterfeiting is a mass-market reality."
- **Source:** Widely reported Nigerian media coverage of Standards Organisation of Nigeria statements; dispute covered in same reporting.
- **Devpost phrasing:** "Even discounting the disputed SON 80% figure, every source — including traders themselves — agrees counterfeits are pervasive." ⚠️ Use as color, never as your headline stat. Headline stats must be A–C above.
