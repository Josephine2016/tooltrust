ToolTrust 🔧✅

Verified repair-tools sourcing for the technicians of Computer Village, Ikeja — check authenticity before you pay, compare real prices, restock on WhatsApp in minutes.

Theme: "Create a project that solves an issue in your community." · Lagos, Nigeria 🇳🇬

The problem is documented and local

80%of phones & gadgets at Computer Village flagged fake by SON*
~35%price gap for identical tools between streets
2–4 hrslost per restock trip walking between stalls

*Standard Organisation of Nigeria claim — disputed by traders, but even the dispute confirms counterfeiting is a mass-market reality. Technicians lose money and customers when fake tools fail mid-repair.

Root problem, not symptoms 🌱

❌ SYMPTOM FRAMING

"Technicians buy bad tools because they are careless shoppers."

Blames the victim. Produces nothing useful.

✅ ROOT-CAUSE FRAMING

The market has no trusted, low-effort way to verify authenticity before payment or compare prices across sellers. Trust is purely personal ("my guy") — so fakes thrive and prices are arbitrary.

Fix the trust infrastructure, and the symptoms collapse.

Clear, testable problem statements 🎯

P1 — Verification gap. A technician cannot confirm a tool is genuine before paying. Test: can a buyer get a genuine/not-genuine answer in under 30 seconds? Today: no.

P2 — Price opacity. Identical SKUs vary up to ~35% within the same market with no reference point. Test: quote the same 5 tools on 3 streets, compare.

P3 — Restocking friction. Each restock trip costs 2–4 hours of paid work. Test: time a technician from "I need solder" to "I have solder."

The solution: ToolTrust ⚙️

🛒 Verified catalog

Genuine/OEM-graded tools from vetted suppliers with transparent ₦ prices, search & filters → P2

🔍 Authenticity checker

Deterministic batch-code scheme: checksum + registry → genuine / invalid / not-in-registry in one tap → P1

📲 WhatsApp ordering

Basket → pre-filled WhatsApp message with line items, codes, totals → P3

🚩 Community reports

Flag counterfeit stalls; suppliers risk losing their verified badge → P1 + P2

Why WhatsApp rails? The market already runs on WhatsApp Business — no onboarding, no new payment network needed.

Live demo (2 min) 🎬

  1. Browse catalog → filter "Soldering & Rework", compare ₦ prices
  2. Verify TT-38IN-2210 ✅ genuine vs TT-38IN-9999 ❌ invalid

📱 Works on any ₦10k Android

Single-file HTML · offline-capable · zero accounts · zero backend · GitHub Pages deploy

Technical craft 🛠️

Verification algorithm
checksum(tag) = (Σ (i+3)·charCode(tag[i]) mod 9000) + 1000, cross-checked against a supplier registry.

Fails closed: unknown codes are flagged — never guessed. No "AI magic" theater.

Architecture
Vanilla ES6 · localStorage (local-first) · wa.me deep links · entire app ≈ 26KB for 2G loads.

To production: swap in real supplier WhatsApp line → signed QR batch codes → Paystack escrow.
